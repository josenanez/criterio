# Frequently asked questions · Criterio CRO

Short answers to what people usually ask when they reach the risk family. This file is the only source: the family page on josenanez.com reads them from here.

## What it is

### What do Severo and Casandra do?

Severo puts each exposure the areas report next to its limit and its plan, and says which limit has no owner or is past its review, which high risk in the register has no limit or indicator and whether the board reviewed the appetite statement within the framework's period. Casandra puts each key indicator next to its threshold and each loss event next to its record: the warning with no action, the unreported indicator, the incomplete or late-registered event, the one repeating in the same process and the one missing from the register.

### Do they approve breaches or change limits?

No. No agent sets a limit or a threshold, approves a breach, accepts or rates a risk, or closes an event. They say what is outside and where, with its citation; the decision is the board's, the risk committee's and the areas'.

### Do they calculate capital or value at risk?

No. They process the figures the areas and the organisation's models report: the exposure against its limit, an indicator's value against its threshold and the amounts in the event database. They do not model or replace a model.

### Which frameworks do they work on?

On the ISO 31000 risk management process and, in a supervised entity, its country's regulation: the Financial Superintendency's Integrated Risk Management System in Colombia, SBS Resolution 272-2017 in Peru and CMN Resolution 4,557 in Brazil. They are cited by name, never by their text.

## How it is used

### Does it connect to my risk system?

No. It processes the Excel or CSV exports of the risk register, the limits, the exposure, the indicators and the event database, and the documents the organisation leaves in its folder: the appetite statement, the operational risk policy and the minutes. The setup maps the columns of any export, in Spanish, Portuguese or English.

### Is it useful outside banking?

Yes. It serves any organisation with an appetite framework, limits and an event database. Supervisory regulation is only cited for a supervised entity.

### Who sets the appetite, limits, thresholds and deadlines?

The organisation, in its appetite framework and its operational risk policy. The setup proposes the deadlines from those documents and the person confirms them.

## Status

### Has it been tested?

The engine is tested against all its synthetic material, with a key the agents never see and two control entities with no signal. In 0.2.0, the two agents ran on every entity in the material, with a key they never see. It has not been tested on a specific organisation's documentation.
