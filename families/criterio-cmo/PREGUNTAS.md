# Frequently asked questions · Criterio CMO

Short answers to what people usually ask when they reach the marketing family. This file is the only source: the family page on josenanez.com reads them from here.

## What it is

### What do Lucía and Próspero do?

Lucía puts each claim in an approved piece next to its substantiation and each promotion next to its terms, and says which piece went out without approval or without being identified as advertising and which contact base was used without marketing consent. Próspero puts each campaign next to its budget, goal, end date and media invoices, and each report result next to the platform export.

### Do they approve pieces or judge the creative?

No. No agent approves a piece, judges the creative, says whether a claim is true, approves a budget or decides which of two figures is right. They say what is missing and where, with its citation; the decision is legal's, the marketing office's and that of whoever signs the report.

### Which frameworks do they work on?

On the International Chamber of Commerce Advertising and Marketing Communications Code, for what is advertised, and the Media Rating Council outcomes and data quality standards, for what is measured. For an entity in Colombia, also the Consumer Statute. They are cited by name and article, never by their text.

## How it is used

### Does it connect to Google Ads, Meta or my analytics tool?

No. It processes the Excel or CSV exports of the plan, the media invoices and the platform results, and the documents the organisation leaves in its folder: the advertising policy, the pieces, the substantiation, each promotion's terms and the results report.

### Does it process my customers' data?

No. From a contact base it only processes its register entry: how many records it has, with what consent and since when. If an extract with people's data arrives, it is not opened.

### Who sets the tolerances and who approves?

The organisation, in its advertising policy. The setup proposes the tolerances from that document and the person confirms them; who approved each piece is in the piece register.

## Status

### Has it been tested?

The engine is tested against all its synthetic material, with a key the agents never see and two control entities with no signal. In 0.2.0, the two agents ran on every entity in the material, with a key they never see. It has not been tested on a specific organisation's documentation.
