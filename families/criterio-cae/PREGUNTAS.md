# Frequently asked questions · Criterio CAE

Short answers to what people usually ask when they reach the internal audit family. This file is the single source: the family's page on josenanez.com reads them from here.

## What it is

### What do Prudencia, Justo and Remedios do?

Prudencia puts the audit plan next to the risk matrix and the audit universe and tells you which high risk has no engagement, which unit went past its cycle and which change has no minutes. Justo puts each finding next to its criteria and its working paper, and each report next to its papers. Remedios takes each finding to its closure, with its evidence, and gathers what the audit committee needs to know.

### Do they issue an audit opinion?

No. No agent concludes on internal control, signs a report, changes a finding's priority, closes an action plan or accepts a risk. They say what is missing and where, with the citation; the opinion is the auditor's.

### Which standards do they work on?

The IIA's Global Internal Audit Standards, mandatory since 9 January 2025, and their topical requirements in force. Each signal is tied to its standard, cited by number and title, never by its text.

## How it is used

### Does it connect to my audit tool?

No. It processes the Excel or CSV exports and the documents the organisation leaves in its folder: the plan, the minutes, the programs, the papers, the reports and the findings register. The setup maps the columns of any export.

### Who sets the cycles, the scale and the deadlines?

The organisation, in its audit charter and methodology. The setup proposes them from those documents and the person confirms them.

### Is what they find published in Rostrum?

Only if the configuration says so. By default it is not: working papers belong to the audit function.

## Status

### Is it tested?

The engine is tested against all its synthetic material, with a key the agents never see and two control entities with no signal at all. The round with the three agents comes in 0.2.0, and it has not been tested on a specific organisation's documentation.
