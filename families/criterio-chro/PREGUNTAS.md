# Frequently asked questions · Criterio CHRO

Short answers to what people usually ask when they arrive at the talent family. This file is the only source: the family page on josenanez.com reads them from here.

## What it is

### What do Norma and Cándido do?

Norma puts every job profile next to the occupation in its standard —DANE's CUOC or O*NET— and says what it lacks, which requirement no task supports and what it asks for that cannot be asked for. Cándido puts every claim in the CV next to its supporting document and every candidate next to the profile, requirement by requirement, and checks data authorisation and retention and each vacancy's deadline.

### Do they decide who is hired?

No. No agent rejects, discards, scores or ranks candidates by a number. They present each requirement with its evidence, in candidate identifier order, and the person selecting decides.

### What do they never do?

Use a candidate's photograph, age, sex, marital status, origin, religion or health; look for people on networks; infer personality or potential; or approve a profile.

## How it is used

### Which occupational standard does it use?

The one the organisation chooses: DANE's CUOC 2026 or O*NET 31.0, from their official download in the reference folder.

### Does it connect to my ATS or to LinkedIn?

No. It processes the exports the organisation leaves in the folder. It prepares the search for the recruiter to run in their own search engine.

### Where do the deadlines come from?

From the organisation's selection policy: the time to fill a vacancy and how long data is kept. If the policy does not say, the person is asked.

## The data

### What if a CV includes the photograph or the age?

It is excluded from the analysis: the record keeps only what kind of data arrived and where, never its value, and the report says what was excluded.

### How do I know it does not discriminate?

Through the bias test: pairs of candidates identical except for one protected attribute must give exactly the same result, and a pair that does not blocks the version.

### Where are my documents processed?

On the AI platform's infrastructure, not only on your computer. A CV carries personal data: confirm this is acceptable under your data policy before pointing it at that folder.

## Status

### Is it ready to use?

It is at its version 0.1.0, in the lab, and can be used: the engine is tested on all the material, with the bias test, and both agents passed their first rounds on the Colombian bank and its control, with no false alarm in the control. The rest of the material runs with the agents in the next round.
