## Read this before you install

**Criterio produces working drafts, not decisions, and nothing here has been certified by anyone.**

This repository contains instructions for a language model. Its output must be checked by someone qualified before it is used to decide, sign, file, report or escalate.

It is published under Apache 2.0, **as is, with no warranty of any kind**. Whoever deploys it, tests it or runs it does so on their own responsibility and accepts the [terms of use](TERMS.md).

## What it is and what it is not

| It is | It is not |
|---|---|
| A way to read many documents quickly and point at where to look | A judgement about a project, a person or a decision |
| A method with traceability: every statement carries its source | A source of truth about your organisation |
| A record of what the documents say | A record of what is actually happening |

That last row is the one that matters. Its input is documents. It knows what was written, not what was said in a corridor or decided in a call nobody minuted. Every finding it produces is "according to the documents", and it says so.

## Limits you must know

**1. The model makes mistakes.** It can misread a date, miss a section, or over-interpret. That is why every extracted data point carries a citation to the source document and declares when it did not find the data. Check against the source before acting.

**2. Silence is not evidence.** A project with no documentation is not badly run — it is undocumented, and that is all the output can say. Absence of a finding is not proof that nothing is wrong.

**3. Your content is processed on the AI platform's infrastructure**, not only on your machine. Before processing confidential documents or third-party personal data, confirm that this is admissible under your policies, your contracts and the applicable data protection law. That assessment is yours to make, not this project's.

**4. No warranty, no liability.** Under Apache 2.0 this work is provided "as is", and neither the maintainer nor any contributor is liable for damages arising from its use.

## Personal data and confidentiality

This repository does not and must not contain client documents, employer data, confidential process information, or real personal data. All test material is synthetic.

If you contribute an example, build it from scratch. Do not anonymise a real document: anonymisation fails.

## If you are going to rely on this

Do not. Rely on the person who reads the output.

The value of Criterio is that someone who would have read twelve documents in a week reads the summary of two hundred in an afternoon and knows where to look. It is triage, not judgement.
