# Disclaimer

[Español](DISCLAIMER.es.md)

## Read this before you install

**Criterio gives management analysis tested against the evidence: every data point with a citation to the document it comes from. Every version goes through an internal validation before it is published, and its evidence is public.**

It is run by a language model (LLM) on the documents you point it to. What it delivers is the input to the decision, not the decision: before using it to decide, sign, file, report or escalate, whoever signs verifies it against its sources.

It is published under the Apache 2.0 licence, which governs its warranty and liability. Whoever deploys it, tests it or runs it accepts the [terms of use](TERMS.md).

## What it is and what it is not

| It is | It is not |
|---|---|
| The analysis of a function's whole documentation, tested against the evidence, with the source of every data point | The judgement on a project, a person or a decision, which belongs to whoever leads |
| A method with traceability: every statement carries its source, and what is not written is said | A source of truth about your organisation |
| What the documents say, complete and comparable | What was agreed outside them |

That last row sets the scope. Its input is documents: it knows what was written, not what was said in a meeting nobody recorded. Every finding is "according to the documents", and it says so.

## Limits you must know

**1. The language model can be wrong.** It can process a date wrongly, miss a section, or over-interpret. That is why every extracted data point carries a citation to the source document and declares when it did not find the data. It is checked against the source before acting.

**2. The absence of a finding is not a conclusion.** A project with no documentation is not badly run — it is undocumented, and that is what the analysis says. Absence of a finding is not proof that nothing is wrong.

**3. Your content is processed on the AI platform's infrastructure**, not only on your machine. Before processing confidential documents or third-party personal data, confirm that this is admissible under your policies, your contracts and the applicable data protection law. That assessment is yours to make, not this project's.

**4. Warranty and liability.** Governed by the Apache 2.0 licence, sections 7 and 8.

## Personal data and confidentiality

This repository does not and must not contain client documents, employer data, confidential process information, or real personal data. All test material is synthetic.

If you contribute an example, build it from scratch. Do not anonymise a real document: anonymisation fails.

## How it is used

As the input management requires before deciding: complete, sourced and verifiable. Whoever used to
review twelve documents in a week has the analysis of two hundred in an afternoon, with every figure
tested and every contradiction in view. The decision, and the responsibility for it, remain with
whoever signs it.
