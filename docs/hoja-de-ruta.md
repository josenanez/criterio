# Criterio's roadmap

[Español](hoja-de-ruta.es.md)

Every family in one document. It is generated by `scripts/hoja.py` from each family's roadmap (`families/criterio-<sigla>/hoja-de-ruta.json`), the version register (`registro/versiones.json`) and the scope decisions (`docs/decisions/`): it is not edited by hand.

- **Each family carries its own version** (decision 0017) and only goes up when it changes.
- **Ten versions per whole number** (decision 0020): from x.0 to x.9, and then x+1.0. 0.10, 0.11 and 0.12 are the only exceptions: whoever is there goes up to 1.0.
- **Laboratory or stable is said by the evidence, not the number:** stable if each entity's most recent run in the material has the floor of the 95% margin at 95% or more, for signals and data, and no control raises a signal; otherwise laboratory. `scripts/calidad.py` calculates it.
- **An increment is a version with its full test** and its statistical analysis, with the same metrics as the earlier ones.
