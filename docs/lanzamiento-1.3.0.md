# Criterio · catalogue 1.3.0 · 29 October 2026

The CFO family opens its 1.0.0: it has been launched since the week of 26 October and it is stable. The CLO also reaches its 1.0.0, under the 0.12 exception. Both enter Brazil, with their documents in Portuguese. Open source under Apache 2.0.

**The catalogue number only orders the entries of the version register.** Each family carries its own version.

## What it brings

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brazil, the sixth country** | It enters the country registry with its electronic invoice: the NF-e reader, verified field by field against MOC 7.0, Annex I; the CNPJ with its two check digits; ICMS inside the invoice value, with São Paulo's general rate (RICMS/SP, article 52); and the IFRS Foundation's profile of Brazil |
| **Documents in Portuguese** | Every family's engine processes a Brazilian company's documents like those of the other countries: table headers, months, file names (livro razão, balancete, extrato) and values (concluída, entregue, ativo, mensal) |
| **CFO 1.0.0 · Clara, Tomás and Áurea** | Round 7, in Brazil: retail and mining from São Paulo and a control, with their official NF-e. 24 of 24 signals, none extra, and 24 of 25 data points. With the six rounds across six countries, the lower bound of the 95% margin is 97.6% for signals and 96.2% for data |
| **CLO 1.0.0 · Fidel and Constanza** | Round 6, in Brazil: a bank, a retailer and a control, with contracts, amendments, insurance policies, company certificates, minutes and the compliance report in Portuguese. 20 of 20 signals, none extra, and 395 of 396 data points. With all its rounds, the lower bound is 96.5% for signals and 99.6% for data |

## If you already have it installed

Update `criterio-finance`, `criterio-budget`, `criterio-treasury`, `criterio-legal` and `criterio-compliance`. Each agent asks you to accept the terms of its new version and leaves its receipt in `.criterio/terminos/`.
