# Criterio command manual

The 83 commands of the PMO, CFO, CLO and EA families and Rostrum. First the PMO, in the order it uses them: Alba defines the product, Samuel runs a project and Vera consolidates the portfolio; then the CFO family —Clara closes the books, Tomás runs the budget and Áurea treasury—, the CLO family —Fidel handles the contracts and Constanza compliance— and the EA family —Ofelia prepares the executive's office—; and at the end Rostrum, which publishes all of them. Each command is typed with its plugin prefix; autocomplete does the rest.

This manual is generated by `scripts/manual.py` from each plugin's README table and its `contratos.json`. The full contract of each command —what it reads, shows and writes— is in Spanish, the language the contract is written and tested in: see [manual.es.md](manual.es.md). Do not edit by hand.

## Alba · a product

### `/criterio-product:product-setup`

**What it does.** **The first thing you run.** Looks at your folder, asks four questions and contrasts the definition you already have

**Skills.** **demand-evidence**, **document-intake**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-discovery`

**What it does.** Interviews and tickets into themes with the citation of who said it, and the theme that has gone months said without anybody turning it into anything

**Skills.** **demand-evidence**, **discovery-synthesis**, **document-intake**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-requirements`

**What it does.** The register with its gaps: no owner, no acceptance criteria, accepted without anybody asking for it, and what nobody decides

**Skills.** **product-health**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-definition`

**What it does.** The definition against the evidence of demand, and where the business and the data do not match

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **demand-evidence**, **product-metrics**, **raid-taxonomy**, **regulatory-sweep**

### `/criterio-product:product-trace`

**What it does.** Requirement → decision → project → deliverable, and the two gaps above

**Skills.** **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-spec`

**What it does.** The draft specification with verifiable criteria and the gaps pointed out, not filled in

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **regulatory-sweep**, **requirement-record**, **specification-draft**

### `/criterio-product:product-charter`

**What it does.** The project charter: where the record is born and the writer changes hands

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **demand-evidence**, **governance-artifacts**, **project-record**, **raid-taxonomy**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-business-case`

**What it does.** The business case's structure with every figure cited, and the gaps with who produces them

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-publish`

**What it does.** Publishes your record where the other products can read it

### `/criterio-product:product-overlap`

**What it does.** Where you step on another product: the same metric counted twice, the same project, the same segment

**Skills.** **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-wake`

**What it does.** **The one you put on a clock.** What crossed a threshold without anybody doing anything

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **document-intake**, **product-health**
