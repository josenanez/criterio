# Criterio command manual

The family's 37 commands, in the order a PMO uses them: Alba defines the product, Samuel runs a project, Vera consolidates the portfolio and Rostrum publishes it. Each command is typed with its plugin prefix; autocomplete does the rest.

This manual is generated by `scripts/manual.py` from each plugin's README table and its `contratos.json`. The full contract of each command —what it reads, shows and writes— is in Spanish, the language the contract is written and tested in: see [manual.es.md](manual.es.md). Do not edit by hand.

## Alba · a product

### `/criterio-product:product-setup`

**What it does.** **The first thing you run.** Looks at your folder, asks four questions and contrasts the definition you already have

**Skills.** **demand-evidence**, **document-intake**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-discovery`

**What it does.** Interviews and tickets into themes with the citation of who said it, and the theme that has gone months said without anybody turning it into anything

**Skills.** **demand-evidence**, **discovery-synthesis**, **document-intake**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-requirements`

**What it does.** The register with its gaps: no owner, no acceptance criteria, accepted without anybody asking for it, and what nobody decides

**Skills.** **product-health**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-definition`

**What it does.** The definition against the evidence of demand, and where the business and the data do not match

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **demand-evidence**, **product-metrics**, **raid-taxonomy**, **regulatory-sweep**

### `/criterio-product:product-trace`

**What it does.** Requirement → decision → project → deliverable, and the two gaps above

**Skills.** **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-spec`

**What it does.** The draft specification with verifiable criteria and the gaps pointed out, not filled in

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **regulatory-sweep**, **requirement-record**, **specification-draft**

### `/criterio-product:product-charter`

**What it does.** The project charter: where the record is born and the writer changes hands

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **demand-evidence**, **governance-artifacts**, **project-record**, **raid-taxonomy**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-business-case`

**What it does.** The business case's structure with every figure cited, and the gaps with who produces them

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-publish`

**What it does.** Publishes your record where the other products can read it

### `/criterio-product:product-overlap`

**What it does.** Where you step on another product: the same metric counted twice, the same project, the same segment

**Skills.** **product-metrics**, **requirement-record**

### `/criterio-product:product-wake`

**What it does.** **The one you put on a clock.** What crossed a threshold without anybody doing anything

**Skills.** **assumption-tracking**, **document-intake**, **product-health**

## Samuel · a project

### `/criterio-project:pm-setup`

**What it does.** **The first thing you run.** Looks at your folder, asks four questions and reads your last minutes

**Skills.** **commitment-tracking**, **project-record**

### `/criterio-project:pm-agenda`

**What it does.** The agenda with the items that need somebody in the room, and with what this meeting cannot move

**Skills.** **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-project:pm-minutes`

**What it does.** The minutes over the transcript or the notes, with everything attributed to a person

**Skills.** **commitment-tracking**, **document-intake**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-project:pm-commitments`

**What it does.** Who promised what, what fell due with no evidence, and what keeps being rescheduled meeting after meeting

**Skills.** **commitment-tracking**

### `/criterio-project:pm-report`

**What it does.** The full weekly report **except the status**, which you declare

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**

### `/criterio-project:pm-publish`

**What it does.** Publishes your record where the PMO can read it

**Skills.** **project-record**

### `/criterio-project:pm-plan`

**What it does.** The first draft of the plan and the WBS from the charter, **without committing to any date**

**Skills.** **governance-artifacts**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-project:pm-escalate`

**What it does.** What exceeds your authority, as a closed question, with who it reaches computed

**Skills.** **governance-artifacts**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-project:pm-wake`

**What it does.** **The one you put on a clock.** Looks at what is due according to your meeting, and if nothing is due it stays quiet

**Skills.** **commitment-tracking**, **document-intake**

## Vera and Rostrum · the portfolio

### `/criterio-portfolio:portfolio-setup`

**What it does.** **The first thing you run.** Looks at your folders, asks five questions and produces the first report over your own documents

**Skills.** **portfolio-health**, **project-record**

### `/criterio-portfolio:portfolio-wake`

**What it does.** **What the clock invokes.** Looks at what is due today, does it, and if nothing is due it stays quiet

**Skills.** **document-intake**, **portfolio-health**

### `/criterio-portfolio:portfolio-server`

**What it does.** Raises **Rostrum**, the server: exposes the report for whoever will not open a folder, and says what to ask the organisation for

### `/criterio-portfolio:document-index`

**What it does.** Which documents really changed, what has to be re-read and which citations stopped resolving

**Skills.** **document-intake**, **project-record**

### `/criterio-portfolio:portfolio-scan`

**What it does.** Reads the folder and produces or updates one record per project. The front door

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **commitment-tracking**, **document-intake**, **project-record**, **raid-taxonomy**, **vendor-control**

### `/criterio-portfolio:portfolio-report`

**What it does.** Consolidated report: what changed, what contradicts what, what is quiet, what has no support

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **portfolio-health**

### `/criterio-portfolio:status-report`

**What it does.** A project's status, and the signals its declared traffic light does not explain

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **commitment-tracking**, **project-record**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-portfolio:health-check`

**What it does.** Diagnoses a project from scratch against the evidence, assuming nothing from its report

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **commitment-tracking**, **governance-artifacts**, **project-diagnosis**, **project-record**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-portfolio:project-history`

**What it does.** What happened in a project, with the timeline and since when what was declared stopped holding

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **commitment-tracking**, **portfolio-history**

### `/criterio-portfolio:steering-pack`

**What it does.** Committee material as a pack of decisions, not as a progress report

**Skills.** **governance-artifacts**, **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-portfolio:raid-log`

**What it does.** Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies, including the ones said and never recorded

**Skills.** **raid-taxonomy**

### `/criterio-portfolio:change-control`

**What it does.** Assesses a change in scope, time and cost, computes who it reaches, and creates a new baseline without erasing the previous one

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **governance-artifacts**

### `/criterio-portfolio:budget-tracking`

**What it does.** Approved, committed, spent and forecast, with variance against both baselines

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**

### `/criterio-portfolio:vendor-tracking`

**What it does.** Contractual deliverables against evidence of receipt and against invoicing

**Skills.** **commitment-tracking**, **project-record**, **vendor-control**

### `/criterio-portfolio:product-view`

**What it does.** A product's state across all the projects building it

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**

### `/criterio-portfolio:project-charter`

**What it does.** Reviews or drafts the charter, pointing out what is missing and what follows from that

**Skills.** **governance-artifacts**

### `/criterio-portfolio:project-closure`

**What it does.** Closes against the agreed success criteria, with lessons that can be supported

**Skills.** **baseline-variance**, **governance-artifacts**
