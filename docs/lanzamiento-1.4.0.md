# Criterio · catalogue 1.4.0 · 30 October 2026

The sales family changes its name: it is the **CCO · The commercial function**, for Chief Commercial Officer (decision 0022). It opens its 0.3.0 in Ecuador, Peru and Chile, with the Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Zoho CRM exports, and becomes stable. Open source under Apache 2.0.

**The catalogue number only orders the entries of the version register.** Each family carries its own version.

## What it brings

| | |
|---|---|
| **CCO, the family's name** | It was CSO, an acronym that also reads as Chief Security Officer, the security family that comes next. Entries 1.0.0 to 1.3.0 of the register stay sealed as written; the register reads their CSO 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 as the CCO |
| **Dynamics 365 Sales and Zoho CRM** | The engine processes Dynamics' Excel export by each column's label or logical name, with the status —open, won or lost— above the stage (F-06, F-07). It also processes Zoho's deals export by the label of each standard field (F-08, F-09). Every field, verified at its source |
| **Ecuador, Peru and Chile** | Material for two industries and a control per country, with Ecuador's and Peru's RUC and Chile's RUT with its check digit: 117 seeded signals |
| **CCO 0.3.0 · Augusto and Valeria** | Round 2, on the nine entities: 117 of 117 signals and 45 of 45 data items on the first run, with one extra signal in Peru's control. It was Zoho's last activity, which is not among its standard fields: the engine now asks about it and the setup records it (CCO-4), and the verification came out with none. With both rounds, the 95 % margin floor is 98.0 % in signals and 95.1 % in data: stable |

## If you already have it installed

Update `criterio-sales` and `criterio-revenue`. Each agent asks you to accept the terms of its new version and leaves its receipt in `.criterio/terminos/`. Your configuration stays in `.criterio/comercial` and `.criterio/ingresos`: the name change does not touch it.
